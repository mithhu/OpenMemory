# A 90-second OpenMemory demo

## The opening — 10 seconds

“Most memory apps help you collect things. OpenMemory helps you notice what those things mean together.”

Show the constellation. Explain that each point is an ordinary note from a fictional demo story. Astra maps relationships automatically; dashed links are possible connections. The initial briefing is labeled as an example until you generate a live one.

## The reveal — 25 seconds

Click **Find the hidden threads**. While Astra works, open **Dad's birthday next week** and show the sentence: he doesn't need more things; he misses your long walks.

Open **The walk I nearly skipped**. The notes live separately, but together they suggest a birthday plan grounded in something that already mattered.

Once the live result appears, show its ASTRA badge. Click a source chip on a finding to open the original memory. The generated wording will vary; use the actual live result rather than promising an exact phrase.

“Every insight has receipts. If the evidence is thin, Astra says so.”

## The reasoning — 20 seconds

Go to **Ask your memory** and use **What should I make time for this week?**, or ask “What would make Dad's birthday meaningful, based on what I already know?”

Show the answer and its evidence. Highlight that it combines personal details and competing priorities rather than offering generic advice.

## The loop — 20 seconds

Explain that completing a suggested step creates a memory of the explicit completion status. Demonstrate with a clearly fictional demo action and open the resulting **A step taken** memory.

“Understanding changes what you do. What you do becomes new context. Your memory companion grows with the story.”

Reopen the action if demonstrating rather than actually doing it. The history is retained.

## The close — 15 seconds

Capture a short note: “I called Dad. He wants to walk by the river on Saturday and grab coffee afterwards.”

Show that Astra merges it into an existing memory or asks you to clarify if the subject is ambiguous. The map updates automatically; use **Find the hidden threads** to generate a new briefing from the changed context.

“OpenMemory turns remembering into understanding—and understanding into a small next step.”

## Before you present

- Keep the local server running and verify Astra is enabled.
- Generate one live briefing in advance; it persists through refreshes. API requests can take time.
- Keep an exported JSON backup of any real memories.
- Use the demo story for judging rather than sharing private personal notes.
- Mark preview results honestly. The live ASTRA badge appears only after a successful API response.
- Real calendar events, notifications, and messages aren't sent by the app. Next steps are proposals for the person.
