# Label review before predictions

132 training notes: 100 newly authored fictional notes plus the existing 32 development examples. 48 newly authored qualification notes are reserved from training; 16 series and 32 retention across all eleven categories. No public notes or private user memories are included.

Reviewed by the same AI author against the v5 policy, not independently human adjudicated. Reviewed identity and action separately, ordinary read/watch/listen controls, partial versus whole-series completion, negation, future starts, vague references, and independent mixed events. Exact note overlap is absent within the combined data and against prior TV JSON note collections. Qualification work titles differ from the training mixture; initial draft used The Bear, noticed it was in historical TV material, and replaced it before any training/predictions. Other historical vocabulary, semantic families, and pretrained knowledge can overlap. This is fresh qualification for this attempt, not a final independent benchmark.

Important policy choices: past watched/read alone does not prove complete; episode/season completion is progress; known media requests/reactions keep their action while missing identity; ordinary tastes stay NOTE; a person's standalone preference stays PERSON. Open-ended film suggestions have missing_identity. No note is relabeled after observing predictions.

Schema checks validate shapes, not semantic truth. All eleven categories occur in both splits. Counts and scope are frozen in recipe.json, alongside hashes of code, labels, and the contract. Historical v3 gold/results remain untouched.
